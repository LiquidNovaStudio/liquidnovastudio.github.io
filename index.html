About

LiquidNova Studio, LLC is an independent game and app development studio based in Carlisle, Ohio. We design and build our own mobile titles — puzzle games, strategy games, and focused utility apps — and publish them under the LiquidNova Studio name on the App Store and Google Play.

In Development

Tidy Trucks Puzzle Game

A jam-and-sort puzzle game: clear a crowded loading dock by sending each truck out in the right order, with a head-to-head duel mode on identical layouts.

PokerSuite Poker Bankroll App

A bankroll manager for live and online poker players, with session tracking, deep statistics, a stakes ladder, and study tools.

Tower Rivals Tower Defense

A tower-defense battler: raise your towers, hold the line against waves of enemies, and face other players in ranked, casual, and friend duels.

Match-3 Duel Game Puzzle Game

A germ-clearing match-3: swap, match, and clear every germ off the board level by level — then take the same skills into head-to-head duels. Title to be announced.
